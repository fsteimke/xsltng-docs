  2. Specify the exact version when installing:

    >sudo zypper in kernel-default-6.4.0-150600.9.2

  3. When uninstalling a kernel, use the commands zypper se -si 'kernel*' to list all kernels installed and zypper rmPACKAGENAME-VERSION to remove the package.
