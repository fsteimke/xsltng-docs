2.9. Controlling your desktop’s power management

You can open the power settings by opening the Activities overview by pressing Meta and typing power. Now select SettingsPower from the results.

On certain events, such as a critically low battery state, GNOME displays notifications informing you about the event.

For more information, see the section called “Configuring power settings”.
