17.7. Printing images

To print an image, select FilePrint from the image menu. If your printer is configured in the system, it should appear in the list. You can configure printing options on the Page Setup and Image Settings tabs.

Figure 17.3. The print dialog

When you are satisfied with the settings, click Print. Cancel aborts printing.
