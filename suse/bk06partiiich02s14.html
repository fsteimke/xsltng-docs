22.14. Agent forwarding

By adding the -A option, the ssh-agent authentication mechanism is carried over to the next machine. This way, you can work from different machines without having to enter a password, but only if you have distributed your public key to the destination hosts and properly saved it there. (Refer to the section called “Public key authentication” to learn how to copy your public keys to other hosts.)

AllowAgentForwarding yes is the default in /etc/ssh/sshd_config. Change it to No to disable it.
