5.8. Disabling LVM on boot

If there is an error on the LVM storage, the scanning of LVM volumes may prevent the emergency/rescue shell from being entered. This makes further problem diagnosis impossible. To disable this scanning in case of an LVM storage failure, you can pass the nolvm option on the kernel command line.
